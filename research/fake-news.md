## What Fake News Is

Fake news is false or misleading content made to look like real, credible information. It's designed to spread quickly and be believed — often to cause panic, mislead the public, or damage someone's reputation. In NovaPay's case, the fake post is dressed up to look like a legitimate warning about the company's finances, when it isn't.

## Disinformation

Disinformation is false information created and shared **on purpose**, by someone who knows it isn't true. Whoever posted "NovaPay has collapsed" — if they knew that was false — is the source of disinformation. The goal is usually to cause damage: financial harm, panic, or loss of trust in the company.

## Misinformation

Misinformation is false information passed along by someone who **genuinely believes it's true**. A NovaPay employee or customer who sees the post and shares it to "warn" others isn't lying — they've simply been fooled by the original disinformation. This is how a single fake post can snowball across social media in hours.

## Social Engineering

Social engineering is the manipulation of people — using fear, urgency, or trust — to get them to act without thinking carefully. If the fake NovaPay post included something like "click here to secure your account before it's too late," that's social engineering layered on top of the fake news: using the panic it creates to trick people into clicking a malicious link or giving up their login details.

## Reputation Attacks

A reputation attack is false information deliberately used to damage a company's or person's public image. Even if NovaPay's finances are completely fine, the post itself can cause real harm — customers panicking, withdrawing funds, or losing trust — purely because people believed something untrue.

## How Employees Can Verify Information Before Sharing It

### The Verification Process: STOP → CHECK → VERIFY → CONFIRM → SHARE

* [ ] **STOP** — Pause before clicking, replying, downloading, or sharing.
* [ ] **CHECK** — Examine the sender, source, link, message, and details carefully.
* [ ] **VERIFY** — Confirm the information using a trusted and independent source.
* [ ] **CONFIRM** — Make sure the information is accurate and the action is safe.
* [ ] **SHARE** — Only share the information after it has been verified.


---

## Recommended Measures for NovaPay

- **Issue an official statement** quickly through verified company channels to correct the record
- **Contact customers directly** (email, app notification) so they hear the truth from NovaPay first, not rumors
- **Monitor social media** for early mentions of the brand so false claims can be caught fast
- **Report fake or impersonating accounts** to the platforms hosting them
- **Train employees** on the STOP → CHECK → VERIFY → CONFIRM → SHARE process, so staff don't accidentally spread the disinformation themselves
- **Stay transparent** — openly explaining what actually happened builds more trust than staying silent
