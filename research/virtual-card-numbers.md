## What a Virtual Card Number Is

A **Virtual Card Number (VCN)** is a temporary, randomly generated card number that's linked to your real bank account or credit card, but isn't the real number itself. You use it to pay for things online, while your actual card number stays hidden and protected.

Think of it like a disposable mask for your real card — it works for payments, but if it ever leaks or gets stolen, your actual card is untouched.

## How It Works

1. You generate a virtual card number through your bank or a card provider's app
2. It's linked behind the scenes to your real card or account
3. You use the virtual number to pay online, just like a normal card
4. The payment goes through and gets charged to your real account
5. If the virtual number is ever stolen or misused, you simply deactivate it — your real card stays safe and doesn't need to be cancelled

## How It Differs From a Physical Card

| Physical Card | Virtual Card |
|---|---|
| Fixed number, same every time | Number can change with each use or merchant |
| If leaked, entire card must be cancelled/reissued | If leaked, just disable that one virtual number |
| Used both online and in-store | Used online only |
| One card = one number | One real card can generate many virtual numbers |

## Single-Use Virtual Cards

A number that works for exactly **one transaction**, then automatically becomes invalid. Even if someone intercepts it afterward, it's useless — it already expired the moment it was used. Great for one-off purchases like a single online order.

## Merchant-Specific Virtual Cards

A number that's locked to work with **only one specific merchant**. If NovaPay creates a virtual card just for a software subscription (say, Adobe), that number will only work for payments to Adobe — even if the number is stolen, it can't be used anywhere else.

## Spending Limits

You can set a maximum amount the virtual card is allowed to spend, either per transaction or overall. Even if the number falls into the wrong hands, the damage is capped — an attacker can't rack up unlimited charges.

## Expiry Controls

You can set the virtual card to automatically expire after a certain date or time — even if it's not stolen or misused, it simply stops working on its own after the set period, closing the window of risk.

## How Virtual Cards Could Help NovaPay

- **Reduces exposure of the real company card** — if a virtual number is leaked in a breach, NovaPay's main payment card details stay safe
- **Limits damage automatically** — spending caps mean even a compromised virtual card can't cause major financial loss
- **Easy to track spending** — merchant-specific cards make it clear exactly which subscription or vendor a charge came from, useful for spotting fraud or unwanted recurring charges
- **Simple to cancel individual cards** without disrupting all other company payments, unlike replacing a physical card (which affects every service tied to it)
- **Reduces risk from recurring software subscriptions** — since NovaPay regularly buys software/services online, using merchant-locked virtual cards for each one limits how much any single leak can expose

## Limitations

- **Online use only** — can't be used for physical, in-person purchases
- **Still requires a real card/account behind it** — a VCN doesn't eliminate risk entirely, it just adds a layer of protection
- **Some merchants don't accept virtual cards**, especially ones that require matching billing details tightly
- **Doesn't protect against all fraud types** — for example, it won't stop social engineering scams that trick an employee into approving a legitimate-looking payment
- **Requires proper management** — if NovaPay doesn't track and deactivate old virtual cards, unused ones could still pose a lingering risk
- **Doesn't replace other security controls** — still needs to be combined with MFA, employee training, and monitoring, not used as a stand-alone fix
